
## 2026-03-31 - [Micro-UX Constraint & Actionable Contact Info]
**Learning:** Prioritize targeted, high-impact UX improvements (like making static contact info actionable) over broad component refactors to stay within 'micro-UX' scope and line limits. For multiple contact values (e.g., dual WhatsApp numbers), using a wrapping container with individual interactive links ensures each is independently reachable and accessible.
**Action:** Identify the single most friction-inducing element in a section and apply a focused fix. Use 'flex-wrap' and consistent focus rings ('rounded px-0.5') for inline text links to ensure a clean, accessible UI.
