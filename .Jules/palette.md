## 2025-05-14 - [Contact Form Accessibility & Interactivity]
**Learning:** For 'placeholder-only' form designs, use Shadcn UI components with `sr-only` labels and explicit `id`/`htmlFor` associations to maintain visual minimalism while ensuring screen reader accessibility. Concurrently, converting static contact text (email/WhatsApp) into interactive links with proper focus states enhances both usability and navigation delight.
**Action:** Always provide hidden labels for placeholder-only inputs and linkify contact details using `mailto:` and `wa.me` prefixes.
