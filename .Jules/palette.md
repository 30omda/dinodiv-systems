## 2026-04-14 - Interactive Contact Information
**Learning:** Converting static contact strings (email, WhatsApp) into interactive links with proper focus rings and hover states significantly improves the "flow" and accessibility for users on both mobile and desktop. For dual contact values, splitting them into individual links ensures each is independently reachable.
**Action:** Always check for opportunities to convert static contact data into actionable links using `mailto:` and `wa.me/` formats.
