## 2024-04-11 - [Actionable Contact Info]
**Learning:** Static contact information (email, WhatsApp) in sections like 'Contact' should always be converted to interactive links (`mailto:`, `wa.me/`) to reduce friction for users attempting to reach out. For multiple values (like dual WhatsApp numbers), providing individual interactive links within a wrapping flex container ensures each remains independently reachable.
**Action:** Always scan contact sections for static text-based reach-out methods and convert them to their interactive URI equivalents.
