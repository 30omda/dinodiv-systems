## 2025-05-14 - [Interactive Contact Links & Focus Polish]
**Learning:** For a more intuitive and accessible contact section, static text like email and phone numbers should be converted into interactive links (mailto:, wa.me/). When these links are inline or clustered, adding 'rounded px-0.5' to the focus ring style ensures the indicator doesn't clip and provides better visual breathing room.
**Action:** Always wrap contact details in appropriate protocol links and apply 'rounded px-0.5 focus-visible:ring-1' for a polished keyboard navigation experience.
