## 2025-05-14 - Contact Form Accessibility and Interactivity
**Learning:** Placeholders are not a substitute for labels. Even in minimal designs, adding `sr-only` labels ensures screen reader accessibility without affecting visual aesthetics. Linking contact info (Email, WhatsApp) significantly reduces friction for users.
**Action:** Always provide hidden labels for "placeholder-only" inputs and ensure contact information is actionable with appropriate URI schemes (`mailto:`, `wa.me/`).
