## 2025-05-15 - [Interactive Contact Links]
**Learning:** Converting static contact information (email, phone, WhatsApp) into interactive links significantly improves usability. Using `wa.me` for WhatsApp and `mailto:` for email are standard patterns. For multiple values (e.g., two WhatsApp numbers), providing individual links in a wrapping container ensures each is independently actionable and accessible.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to convert static contact strings into actionable links with appropriate `hover` and `focus-visible` states.
