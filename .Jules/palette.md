## 2025-04-02 - [Interactive Contact Information]
**Learning:** Converting static contact information (email, WhatsApp) into interactive links provides immediate utility and delight. When multiple values are present (e.g., dual WhatsApp numbers), using a wrapping container with `flex-wrap` and individual links ensures each remains independently reachable and accessible.
**Action:** Always look for static contact strings and convert them to actionable protocols (`mailto:`, `wa.me/`, `tel:`) with proper hover and focus-visible states.
